// 01 — About
Introduction

Overview.

I’m Munibullah Shah, an AI automation and backend engineer. I design and run LLM agents that do real work — triaging a company’s inbox over Microsoft Graph, delivering email summaries into a Google Chat workspace, managing live ad accounts, and running conversational intake for medical practices — and I build the backends, retrieval layers and infrastructure underneath them.

My work spans the whole stack of an automation: prompt and context design, the tool layer an agent is allowed to touch, guardrails and confidentiality rules, Node.js / TypeScript and Python services, PostgreSQL and Firestore, cloud identity and IAM, and self-hosted deployment on Docker, Traefik and Dokploy. I define the category rubric an agent is graded against and review its output against it, so “working” is a measurement rather than an impression. When an agent leaks, loops or runs up a bill, I’m the one who finds it and fixes the architecture — not just the prompt.

Before this I spent 5+ years as a Flutter and full-stack engineer, shipping 10+ production apps to the App Store and Google Play — including a government emergency-response platform with 500K+ downloads. That delivery record is why the automation work ships instead of staying a demo.
